## Self-review conclusion

The implementation makes the relevant method visible at the decision point,
provides current tool mappings, reduces verified discovery duplication and gives
existing artifacts a concise record of the chosen method. It deliberately does
not claim to enforce all semantic decisions: advisory Knowledge cannot guarantee
that an agent chooses the best representation. Deterministic tools validate their
supported constraints; the primary agent still judges the rendered result.
Unsupported capability is reported explicitly, and no successful product write
is replayed because description delivery or Knowledge retrieval failed.
