## Boundary

The primary agent chooses interpretations and judges appearance. The tools validate
supplied inputs, issue identities and execute their own contracts regardless of
where the decisions came from. Tests never submit a Knowledge compliance token.
App guidance contains names, fields and recovery; the portable domain module does
not. Flat/stylized and known-value tasks do not inherit architectural requirements.

The new integrated test executes real preparation, canonical admission, discovery,
operation resolution, reference and review owners. It controls the primary-agent
verdict, image availability, browser mutation acknowledgement and source-change
notification. It is not a pixel/rendering, persistence, real-browser or model test.
Existing consumer suites additionally exercise retained-query cancellation,
leases, stale-target rejection, partial/unknown settlement and missing-input repair.
No new behavior was added to those consumers.
