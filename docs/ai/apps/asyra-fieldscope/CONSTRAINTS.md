# Constraints

The four connected bays are fixed in count. Editable length is 2–200 m, bay width 2–20 m, height 2–10 m, and strip count 1–32. Strip widths are 0.05–20 m; equal remaining margins must be at least 0.02 m. Circular roof rise cannot exceed half the span. Configuration validation also bounds support count and requires room for supports and plant roots in adjacent soil.

This is a geometric visualization. It does not certify greenhouse construction, predict plant growth, simulate water flow, detect collisions, assess soil stability, calculate robot balance, or validate harvesting damage. Crop variants are original visual models informed by cultivar references; modeled leaf dimensions and plant heights are illustrative.

No backend, robot connection, export, saved project loading, or deployment credentials are introduced. Plant arrangement follows the configured rows; dense foliage overlap is not a collision result. Random appearance is deterministic within a configuration, so view operations and history replay do not shuffle plants.
