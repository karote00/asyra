# Product scope

FieldScope provides a spatial greenhouse workspace for a future harvesting-robot monitoring device. Users first configure the physical growing environment and inspect it with an unrestricted camera.

The eventual product may display robot telemetry, simulate routes and arm motion, evaluate crop damage and plant entanglement, assess terrain stability, and coordinate harvesting containers. Those capabilities require separate measured models and are not implemented by greenhouse geometry or crop appearance alone.

The present scope is connected greenhouses, editable soil/water strips, growing supports and netting, cultivar visualization, camera navigation, and reversible configuration edits. Future support systems may include alternative nets, bamboo, and curved steel; only the current pole-and-net arrangement is implemented.
