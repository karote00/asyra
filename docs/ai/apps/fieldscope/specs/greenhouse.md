# Greenhouse geometry

Four connected bays default to 7 m wide, 50 m long, and 5 m high. Eaves are 60% of height; circular arch rise is the remaining 40%. For span W and rise R, circle radius is `W² / (8R) + R / 2`. Arches are placed every metre and structural post/beam stations every 5 m, including endpoints. Shared bay boundaries share posts. The default model has 204 arches and 55 posts.

Each bay's typed strip sequence is soil 0.9, drain 0.3, soil 1.8, drain 0.3, soil 1.8, drain 0.3, soil 0.9 m. It totals 6.3 m, leaving 0.35 m on each side. Shared edges form a 0.7 m passage. Black rigid waterproof barriers are only at the two outermost sides.

Plastic film covers the roofs and exterior sides and ends, with door openings. Internal shared boundaries have no film partition. Film visibility and opacity are presentation controls.

## Growing supports

A 20 mm pole stands in soil on each soil-adjacent side of a drain, initially 0.15 m from its edge. Poles embed 0.15 m and extend 0.15 m above the crossbeam. Longitudinal spacing is 0.60 m, with at least 0.25 m reserved at each end; defaults produce 83 poles per row and 1,992 poles over 24 rows.

Longitudinal 20 mm rails meet the crossing beams tangentially. Spring-wire clips wrap the two crossed pipes, with hooked ends and a sprung bridge; they are not floating decorative brackets. Wire diameter 2.5 mm is a modeling assumption. The original reference is the An-Ja EJ101 crossed-pipe spring clip and the user-provided photograph.

Netting runs longitudinally on the water-facing side of each pole row. Default mesh is 0.15 m square, bottom 0.45 m above soil, top at the 3 m crossbeam, not the extended pole tip. Top cable ties secure the net at every pole. The pole extension remains above the net.
