returned the saved root without another canvas action. The earlier benchmark's
call 843 dimension query was replayed for its three recorded root IDs using
controlled geometry at the recorded dimensions. The reply included one height
and the App kept the complete dimension columns. A failed ordering retained
the successful creation, rejected a request-only artifact ID, and preserved
unknown settlement rather than reporting success. HTTP turns demonstrated a
private save/read with no canvas or model-originated retrieval work. Reviewed
