# Readable visual hierarchy

## What

Use a small, coherent set of text roles: title, section heading, body and supporting label. Typography communicates both reading order and relationships.

## Why

Competing sizes and weights can make a small interface harder to scan. Consistent roles help readers recognize the same meaning across repeated elements.

## How

Start with the actual content and available width. Choose a readable body size, then distinguish headings with spacing, weight and modest size changes. Check long labels, wrapping and the smallest supporting text at the intended display scale. Preserve meaningful text as editable text when the target supports it.

## Provenance

Project-authored synthesis of common design practice. No external source is quoted and no empirical outcome is asserted. Consumer Apps may specialize this advice for their audience and task.

## Evaluation Guidance

Compare hierarchy at normal viewing size. Look for clipped lines, overly dense paragraphs and labels whose role is unclear; adapt the scale to the audience rather than treating a particular size ratio as a rule.
