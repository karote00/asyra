# Consistent and understandable icon use

## What

Use icons to reinforce a recognizable action or category. Prefer one visual family with compatible stroke weight, optical size and corner treatment.

## Why

Mixed icon styles create accidental emphasis. Unfamiliar symbols without labels can obscure the intended action even when the illustration is polished.

## How

Begin with the action label and decide whether an icon adds meaning. Align icons optically with text, leaving space around both. At small sizes simplify detail and inspect the silhouette. Use repeated geometry or an existing icon resource when appropriate; keep the result editable if the task calls for editable artwork.

## Provenance

Project-authored synthesis of common design practice. No external source is quoted and no empirical outcome is asserted. Consumer Apps may specialize this advice for their audience and task.

## Evaluation Guidance

Check recognition at actual display size and compare neighboring icons. A consistent family is a starting point, not a prohibition on intentional stylistic variation. Verify labels and supported interactions separately.
