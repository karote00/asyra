# A landing page with a clear next action

## What

Compose a page around a recognizable offering, useful supporting evidence and a next action. Reuse typography, color, icon and button guidance instead of redefining those topics.

## Why

Visitors need enough context to understand the offer and decide whether to continue. Visual hierarchy helps them find both the promise and the evidence supporting it.

## How

Identify the audience and the actual requested action. Build a headline and short explanation around that intent, then arrange relevant proof and details in a readable sequence. Place the primary action where the surrounding information supports the decision. Adapt the section order to the content rather than filling a fixed template.

## Provenance

Project-authored synthesis of common design practice. No external source is quoted and no empirical outcome is asserted. Consumer Apps may specialize this advice for their audience and task.

## Evaluation Guidance

Review the first viewport and the full page: is the offer understandable, is the action clear, and do supporting sections add evidence? Assess the design against the brief; a strong visual presentation alone does not prove conversion or factual accuracy.
