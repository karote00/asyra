# Color roles and meaningful contrast

## What

Choose colors by role: surface, primary text, secondary text, emphasis and feedback. Keep the number of competing emphasis colors small enough that priority remains visible.

## Why

A color can look attractive in isolation but disappear on its actual background. Semantic roles make repeated components easier to interpret and maintain.

## How

Choose the background first, then inspect text and controls on that background. Use spacing, labels or shape as well as color for important distinctions. When reproducing a reference, sample representative areas and compare the result under the intended background. Keep decorative accents separate from status meanings.

## Provenance

Project-authored synthesis of common design practice. No external source is quoted and no empirical outcome is asserted. Consumer Apps may specialize this advice for their audience and task.

## Evaluation Guidance

Inspect small text, muted labels and controls in context. Consider readability and color-vision differences. These suggestions do not certify accessibility compliance; use the consuming product’s accessibility checks where required.
