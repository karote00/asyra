Opacity is 1 and emission is 0. The whole shell uses one material, including the collar, rim, inner wall, floor, drainage wall and underside. No darker interior material, glazed highlight stripe, painted rings, dirt, normal map, displacement or texture is included.

Reference-sheet contours are annotations only. Do not copy them into texture maps or add grooves. No cast/contact shadows, AO, highlights, glow or directional shading may be baked into surfaces. Runtime scene lighting owns all illumination, shadows and reflections.
