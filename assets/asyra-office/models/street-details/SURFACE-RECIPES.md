# Street Details - Surface Recipes

Use uniform charcoal #383A38 (sRGB), metallic 0.15, roughness 0.80, opacity 1 and emission 0 on every metal surface, including hidden ends and backs. No rust, grain, normal maps or displacement.

The sign's front lettering uses ivory #EEE9DD with the flat mask defined by the JSON strokes. No border. Lettering is paint, not luminous or raised geometry. Use antialiasing only along the mask edge.

No cast/contact shadows, AO, directional gradients, reflections, highlights or glow may be copied into materials. Drawing outlines and white sheet backgrounds are annotations, not asset textures. Runtime lights create the scene's illumination.
