# Surface Recipes

## Ceramic and seat

Uniform sRGB #EEE9DB on every ceramic, seat, lid, and hinge surface. Metallic 0; roughness 0.80; transmission 0; emission 0. The same color applies to cavities, backs, and undersides.

## Hardware

Uniform sRGB #999C99 on flush button, faucet, lever, stem, and drain. Metallic 0.35; roughness 0.65; transmission 0; emission 0. No plated highlights in base color.

## Lighting separation

No cast or contact shadows, ambient occlusion, cavity tint, gradients, reflections, or highlights are texture content. No normal, bump, displacement, or occlusion maps. Use flat unlit colors for asset-sheet review; the application supplies scene lighting later.

Drawing outlines and dimensional annotations are communication marks and must not be copied onto the model. Very slight tonal variation in generated paper/ceramic is not authored texture: the numeric solid colors above are the complete surface recipes.
