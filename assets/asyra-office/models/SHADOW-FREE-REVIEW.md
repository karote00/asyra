## Shared surface rule

Use intrinsic material colours and texture only. Do not bake cast shadows, contact shadows, ambient occlusion, specular highlights, directional shading, glow or a ground plane into asset materials. Runtime scene lighting owns these effects. Contour lines describe geometry; they are not texture instructions. Naturally dark materials remain dark.

These generated drawings are visual references, not calibrated projections or production textures. Existing numeric geometry and surface specifications remain authoritative. The user authorized including these previously uncommitted lighting revisions in the reference handoff. This does not establish model verification.

Transparent road markings remain independent from any road surface. RGB colours in fully transparent pixels are invisible; inspect with alpha compositing enabled.
