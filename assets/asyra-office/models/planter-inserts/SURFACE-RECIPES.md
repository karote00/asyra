Emission and transmission are zero. Planter faces, including cavity and underside, are uniformly the same color. Soil side and bottom faces are uniform.

## Soil top pigment

Use a deterministic sparse speckle field only on the top face. Let fract(x)=x-floor(x), H(i,j)=fract(sin(127.1*i+311.7*j)*43758.5453).

For integer i=0..39 and j=0..15:
- x=-0.273+0.546*(i+0.2+0.6*H(i,j))/40.
- y=-0.103+0.206*(j+0.2+0.6*H(j+17,i+11))/16.
- Dot radius=0.00035+0.00025*H(i+7,j+29).
- Inside each circular dot, mix base toward sRGB #70563F with weight 0.20 in linear RGB after sRGB conversion.

Dots are intrinsic pigment, not cavities, raised particles, shadows or normal detail. Outside dots retain base color. No geometry or displacement is generated by this recipe.

No additional grain, normal, bump, displacement or occlusion maps. Review images are unlit; no cast/contact shadows, AO, gradients, highlights or reflections. Contour strokes are drawing aids. The application supplies scene lighting later.
