Emission and transmission are zero. All assigned faces, including interiors and undersides, use uniform colors.

No writing, grain, paper fibers, page-line texture, label, bump, normal, displacement, or occlusion maps. Reference views are unlit. No shadows, AO, highlights, gradients, reflections, or cavity tint are baked into materials. Drawing contours and labels are not material artwork. The app supplies scene lighting later.
