# Surface Recipes

| Part | sRGB base color | Metallic | Roughness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shrub leaves and grass blades | #81945F | 0 | 0.90 |
| Shrub trunk and branches | #8B6948 | 0 | 0.90 |

Emission and transmission are zero. Use the same color on front, back, edges and hidden surfaces. No leaf veins, bark grain, noise, alpha texture, normal, bump, displacement, or occlusion map.

Use unlit review colors. No cast/contact shadows, AO, highlights, gradients, reflected light, or dark gaps painted into materials. Real spaces between leaves remain open geometry. Contour lines are drawing annotations. Runtime scene lighting supplies depth and shadows later.
