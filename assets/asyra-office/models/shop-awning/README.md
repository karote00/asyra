# Group 4AF - Striped Shop Awning

Status: user-approved reference sheets. Drawings and reconstruction specifications only.

One complete independently placeable awning. Appearance follows the blue-and-ivory canopy on the upper-right bookshop in the [approved office reference](../architecture/review/approved-office-reference.jpg). The central office remains the project's primary source; this shop is a secondary source for this specific component.

The source guides the sloped canopy, striped colors and short straight valance. Exact dimensions, eight-stripe layout, underside and thickness are authored completions rather than recovered measurements.
