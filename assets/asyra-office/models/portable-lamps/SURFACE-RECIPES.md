# Surface Recipes

| Material | sRGB base color | Metallic | Roughness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sage | #899B85 | 0 | 0.80 |
| Ivory | #EEE9DB | 0 | 0.80 |
| Charcoal | #414647 | 0.20 | 0.70 |

Colors are uniform. Emission and transmission are zero for every part, including both bulbs. The shade interiors use the specified intrinsic ivory color without darkening.

No texture, fabric pattern, grain, normal, bump, displacement, or occlusion map. Reference views are unlit. Do not bake shadows, AO, reflections, gradients, highlights, glow or beams. Dark charcoal is the part's color, not a shadow.

Contour lines identify geometry and must not become engraved rings or painted outlines. Runtime light behavior is outside this handoff.
