# Group 4L - Pantry Smallware Review

Status: approved for commit by the user. Drawings and specifications only; no model verification.

1. [Canister](01-pantry-canister-v1.png): one opaque sage jar with a flat wooden lid, based on the central pantry shelf.
2. [Takeaway cup](02-takeaway-cup-v1.png): one tapered cream cup, tan sleeve and ivory lid with one sipping opening, inspired by park seating drinks.

Each sheet has front, right, back, top and oblique views. Review shape, colours, independent unit boundaries and absence of shadows. Flat contour lines are drawing aids, not material textures. Hidden geometry and precise dimensions follow the [geometry supplement](../GEOMETRY-SUPPLEMENT.md) and [JSON](../pantry-smallware-model-spec.json).
