## Review scope

These are supplementary rest-area designs. Review geometry, proportions, view consistency and separate placement boundaries; colours and materials are replaceable defaults. [Geometry](../GEOMETRY-SUPPLEMENT.md), [numeric specification](../rest-furniture-model-spec.json), and [surface recipes](../SURFACE-RECIPES.md) define dimensions and hidden surfaces.

No baked lighting, model output, animation or app implementation. The revised daybed documents are approved by the user for commit.

## Current daybed construction review

The [daybed specification](../DAYBED-SPEC.md) retains this reference and all
primitive dimensions. Seven closed parts have six exact contacts. The rounded
pad has a planar central support and a 10 mm minimum gap from the headboard.
Analytic topology, bounds and LOD checks do not certify a production model.
The separate pillow has not been revised.
