# Group 5E - Yellow and White Garden Flowers

Status: user-approved reference sheets. Drawings and reconstruction specifications only.

Two independent plants: one yellow five-petal flower and one ivory eight-petal flower. Each contains one stem, two leaves, one bloom and one flower center. No pot, soil, grass, buds, extra stems or ground.

The approved garden image supplies small-flower color cues. Exact species, petal count and geometry cannot be recovered at that resolution. These are authored simplified companion plants, not botanical or pixel-exact reconstructions.
