# Surface Recipes

| Surface | Uniform sRGB color |
| --- | --- |
| Stems and leaves | #81945F |
| Yellow petals and white-flower center | #E8C35C |
| Ivory petals | #EEE9DB |
| Yellow-flower center | #B18B45 |

All parts: metallic 0, roughness 0.90, emission 0, transmission 0. Interior/underside faces retain their assigned color.

No veins, pollen pattern, gradient, noise, texture, normal, bump, displacement or occlusion map. Review previews are unlit. No baked shadows, AO, highlights, reflections or glow. Gaps between petals are real space, not painted dark material. Thin sheet contours are annotations. The app supplies lighting later.
