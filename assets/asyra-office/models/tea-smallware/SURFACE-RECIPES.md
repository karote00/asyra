# Tea Smallware - Surface Recipes

Ivory ceramic: sRGB #EEE9DB, metallic 0, roughness 0.80.
Spoon metal: sRGB #B8B8B4, metallic 0.80, roughness 0.65.
Both: opacity 1, emission 0, no normal map or displacement.

Use the same material on tops, undersides, rim faces and the spoon cavity. No brighter lip paint, dark bowl fill, engraved outline or view-dependent colour. The spoon is illustrated in flat grey; runtime metal reflections are produced by the scene, not painted into the asset.

No cast/contact shadows, AO, highlights, reflection streaks, directional gradients or white drawing backgrounds belong in textures. No external texture or font dependencies are required.
