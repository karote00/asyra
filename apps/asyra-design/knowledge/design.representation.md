# Choosing an editable representation

For an existing shape, change the specific property that carries the intent. Equal-looking elements can stay independent. Link properties or elements when future edits should propagate.

A flat contour can be edited as a vector. For repeated details, define the motif once and vary its placements. Use projection when the design depends on world-space geometry and a camera; an existing projected outline can stay in two dimensions. A group follows the bounds of its children. A frame has explicit dimensions and can remain unpainted.

For an isolated object, preserve the requested background. Reference pixels remain outside editable artwork. Reuse suitable existing sources before seeking more. For an illustration based on references, separate observed dimensions from estimates. A small overview can establish broad proportions while a local detail may resolve a material or ornament. Choose the construction order and level of detail from the brief.
